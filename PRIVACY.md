# Contributor Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-11-27

This notice explains how personal data of vPole contributors is collected and used. It covers the
Contributor License Agreement (CLA) process and contributions to this repository. It does not cover
software you deploy with vPole.

## Who is responsible

Victor Shnaider, maintainer of vPole (the "Maintainer"), is responsible for the personal data
described here. Contact: privacy@vpole.cloud.

## What data is collected

When you accept the [CLA](CLA.md) through the CLA bot:

- your GitHub username and account ID;
- your full legal name, email address, and country of residence, which you enter in the CLA form;
- the date and time of acceptance, the CLA version, and the pull request that triggered it.

When you contribute:

- the name and email address recorded in your Git commits;
- your GitHub activity in this repository, such as issues, pull requests, comments, and reviews;
- the content of security reports you submit through GitHub private vulnerability reporting.

## Why it is used

The data is used to:

- document that you granted the licenses in the CLA, which the Maintainer needs to distribute vPole
  under the GNU Affero General Public License and under commercial licenses;
- identify the author of each contribution;
- communicate with you about your contributions and security reports;
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims about rights in vPole.

The legal grounds are the performance of the CLA and the Maintainer's legitimate interest in proving
the rights to distribute vPole. The data is not sold and is not used for marketing.

## Where it is stored

- **CLA records** are stored by [CLA assistant](https://cla-assistant.io), a service operated by SAP
  SE, and may be exported to the Maintainer's own records.
- **Commits, issues, pull requests, and comments** are stored by GitHub and are public. Git history is
  permanent: a commit's author name and email remain in every copy of the repository. To keep your
  email private, use your [GitHub noreply address](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/setting-up-and-managing-your-personal-account-on-github/managing-email-preferences/setting-your-commit-email-address)
  before you commit.

These services may process data outside your country. Their own privacy policies also apply.

## How long it is kept

CLA records are kept for as long as your contributions are included in or distributed with
vPole, and afterwards for as long as they are needed to establish or defend legal claims.
Public contribution data remains in the repository history.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data,
to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. To use these rights, contact
privacy@vpole.cloud.

Some requests cannot be fully met. CLA records prove the licenses you granted and are kept while they
are needed for that purpose. Removing your data from public Git history would require rewriting the
history of every copy of the repository.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Ukraine, this is the Ukrainian Parliament
Commissioner for Human Rights. In the European Union, it is the authority in your country of
residence.
